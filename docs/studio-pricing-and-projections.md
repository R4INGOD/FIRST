# Studio Pricing & Projection Check

**Inputs:**
- Prices in the app (`milestone-app/src/lib/catalog.ts`, approved Oct 20, 2026)
- *Milestone Year 1 Financial Plan v3* (Feb 2026)
- *Milestone Vision Document* (Feb 2026)
- Published Montreal rates (sources at the end)
- Since Feb: **Studio A's control room is not used.** There's no SSL 9000J and no outboard; only the mics, the Prophet Rev2 and the Apollo interfaces remain.

**Capacity basis:** the app books 9:00–21:00 every day. That's **12 h/day, 365 h/month and 4,380 h/year per room.** For a studio, 40% utilization is healthy and 60% is excellent. Anything above that, sustained, is not realistic.

---

## Summary

1. **Studio A's plan is built on the SSL, which is gone.** Studio A was budgeted at $160K in Year 1 and $450K in Year 5 as an SSL room. Year 5 needs 103% utilization even at today's $100/h. Without the console, $100/h is the top of the Montreal market for a tracking room.
2. **Studio B's Year 2–5 targets are physically impossible in one room.** Year 5 ($650K) is about 3× the room's revenue at 100% utilization ($219K).
3. **Studio C's unlimited membership is underpriced and eats the room.** At $345/month unlimited, a member using 12 h/month pays $29/h against $75/h public. Year 5 ($500K) needs 152% utilization at $75.
4. **Studio D (podcast) is the healthiest room.** The season packages work and the Year 1 target is reachable. Self-serve at $125/h is priced above market.
5. **All four rooms together top out around $0.85–0.9M a year at the proposed prices.** The plan has them at $1.95M by Year 5. The plan's $4.35M Year 5 depends on Agency, Video Production and Academy: about $2.4M, rising to about $3.5M if room revenue is capped at its ceiling. Those are people-driven and not live yet (the Academy page says "coming soon").
6. **Break-even is about $36K/month ($433K/year)** on the plan's own costs ($28.5K/month fixed, variable at 21% of revenue). That's reachable from the rooms alone with realistic bookings, so the base business is feasible. The growth story is not, as written.
7. **The plan's cash-flow table understates early losses.** It uses $18K/month fixed costs, but the plan's own fixed costs are $28.5K/month ($17K team + $11.5K overhead). The Months 1–3 hole is about **$45K, not $13K**, before the $35K app build.
8. **Two different Year 1 numbers are in circulation:** $900K in the financial plan and $719K in the May sponsor pitch. Pick one.

## Studio by studio

### Studio A: recording room (ground floor)

**What it is now:** a tracking room with the mic collection, a Prophet Rev2 and Apollo interfaces, run by KJ. There's no console room.

| | Current app price | Market (Montreal / comparable) | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session, engineer included | $100/h, 2 h min | $90 first hour (Porte Parole); $75–85/h with engineer (Toronto); vocal tracking $50–150/h | **$85/h, 2 h min** (revised, see market section) |
| Mixing | $250/song (sold as "on the SSL") | Vision doc range $300–800 was with the SSL | **$200/song** (in the box) |
| Mastering | $100/song | $100–300 (vision doc) | **$100** (keep) |

| Producer / songwriter / musician | $150 / $100 / $100 per hour | n/a | keep |
| **New:** Prophet Rev2 parts, remote | n/a | n/a | **$150/song** (test) |

**Projection check:**
- Plan Year 1 of $160K needs 37% utilization at $100/h, or 43% at $85/h.
- Plan Year 5 of $450K needs 103% at $100/h, or 121% at $85/h.
- Realistic: **$95–110K in Year 1** (25–30% utilization) and a **ceiling of about $185K** (50% utilization at $85/h).
- Anything above that has to come from per-song services (mixing, Prophet parts), not room hours. Those are capped by KJ's time, not the room.

### Studio B: self-serve membership studio (basement)

| | Current | Market | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $275/month, 5 h ($55/h) | Self-serve rooms ~$40–70/h (vision doc) | keep |
| Growth | $500/month, 10 h ($50/h) | | keep |
| Pro | $900/month, 20 h ($45/h) | | keep |
| Extra member hours | $45/h | | keep |
| Non-members | **can't book** | Vision doc planned $40–70/h | **Add $65/h off-peak (weekdays 9–17), 2 h min** |

The prices are fine. **Capacity is the problem.**
- **Year 1 ($160K):** about 27 Growth-level members. At 70% of hours actually used, that's 51% of the room. Ambitious but possible.
- **Year 2 ($340K):** about 57 members using 109% of the room. Impossible.
- **Year 5 ($650K):** 208% of the room. Impossible.
- **Ceiling:** about $131K at 60% utilization, or $219K at 100%.
- **Levers:**
  - Cap memberships at about 30 so members can actually book.
  - Sell daytime hours to non-members.
  - Extend self-serve hours past 21:00. That's the only way to grow this room meaningfully, if ID checks and safety allow it.
- **Open fact:** in February, Marc said Studio B's equipment had been removed. The app still lists an AMEK Mozart and outboard (including an SSL X-Rack). Confirm what's actually in the room.

### Studio C: content creation room (2nd floor)

| | Current | Market | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-serve | $75/h, 2 h min (free for members) | Cyclorama studios $79–90/h; Green Studio from $50/h | keep |
| With an operator | $150/h, 1 h min | | keep |
| Membership | $345/month, **unlimited**, max 14 members | | **$345/month with 10 h included**, then $50/h, still max 14. Or keep unlimited but only weekdays 9–17. |

**Why change the membership:**
- 14 members using 12 h/month each fill 46% of the room and pay an effective $29/h.
- At 20 h/month each, they fill 77% and pay $17/h. That blocks the $75–150/h hourly sales.
- Memberships at their maximum bring in only $58K a year.

**Projection check:**
- Plan Year 1 is $140K over 9 months ($15.6K/month), which needs 57% utilization at $75/h on top of member hours.
- Plan Year 5 is $500K ($41.7K/month), which needs 152% at $75/h or 76% at all-operator $150/h.
- Realistic: **$90–130K for the first full year**, with a **ceiling of about $210K**.
