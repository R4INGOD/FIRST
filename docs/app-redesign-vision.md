# Milestone App: Menu, "Who We Are" and Team Plan (Oct 23, 2026)

Combined from three reviews: UI/UX, marketing and HR. Items marked [CONFIRM] need James.

## 1. The problem
- Too many ways in, with no clear order:
  - The desktop header has 11 links. Between tablet and laptop widths it shows none.
  - The phone menu has 17 entries, and there are 10 action tiles on the home screen.
  - Booking can be reached 8 different ways on a phone.
- The same page goes by different names: /booking is "Studios", "Salles" or "Réserver" depending on where you are; /marketplace is "Marché" or "Emplois".
- Some pages are placeholders: the marketplace has 3 hard-coded listings, the feed and blog never change, the Academy and the virtual tour say "coming soon", and no one ever earns ranks/XP.
- The app never says who Milestone is or shows who works there. The only team text is KJ's bio, on the Studio A page.

## 2. The new menu
Phones get 4 tabs plus a centre **M** button:

| Slot | FR / EN | What's inside |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accueil / Home | home, network strip |
| 2 | Studios | booking, rooms, memberships, gear, tour |
| 3 | **M** | opens the sheet below |
| 4 | Agenda / Events | events, tickets |
| 5 | Moi / Me | profile, my public profile, notifications, settings, staff area |

**The M sheet** slides up from the bottom of the screen. From top to bottom:
1. A search field that works in French or English.
2. **Maintenant / Right now:** your next booking, the next event, and unread notifications.
3. **Faire / Do:** book Studio A, B, C or D; become a member; rent gear; buy beats; get tickets.
4. **Milestone:** Qui on est, Émissions, Nous écrire, Agence ↗.

**On desktop**, the header becomes: Logo · Studios · Agenda · Boutique · search ("⌘K") · bell · avatar · FR/EN. The search opens the same sheet as a command palette.

**Hidden until they're real:** marketplace, feed, blog, academy, ranks. The pages stay but aren't linked or indexed by search engines.

Each destination gets one name everywhere: "Studios" always, never "Salles".

## 5. People and consent (Quebec)
- **Right to one's image** (Civil Code art. 36(5); *Aubry v. Éditions Vice-Versa*) and **Law 25** s. 14 both apply:
  - Get written, opt-in consent before showing a name or photo.
  - Ask for each place separately: app, website, social media.
  - Never pre-tick a box.
  - People can withdraw at any time.
- **Consent can't be a condition of the contract.** Keep it in a separate, optional annex.
- **When someone leaves:** remove their profile within about 10 business days. Credits on released work can usually stay. [Lawyer]
- **Remote editors in Nigeria:** this is a transfer outside Quebec, so it needs a privacy impact assessment (s. 17). [Lawyer]
- **Titles:** avoid employment words like "in-house", "staff", "employé" and "poste". A music producer in Quebec French is a « Réalisateur ». Add the line « Milestone collabore avec des créatifs indépendants ». Titles don't decide contractor status; the PM role is the riskiest. [Accountant]
- **Consent form fields:** display name, title, a 2–3 line bio (FR, then EN), photo plus photographer's credit, links, what they're bookable for. Then 4 separate checkboxes: app / site / social / bookable on the marketplace. Withdrawal by email.
- **« Rejoins le collectif »:** Academy → portfolio review → bookable marketplace profile → studio roster. Collect only name, email, discipline, portfolio and city. No social insurance number or ID.
